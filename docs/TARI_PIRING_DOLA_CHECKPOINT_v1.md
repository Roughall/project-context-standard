## 1. Project and Clip 01 brief

- **Character:** AI-created young Minangkabau Tari Piring dancer (character sheet: age appearance 5 to 7, navy costume with turquoise embroidery, silver suntiang, brooch, bracelet, two white red-floral plates). The character sheet is an identity reference, not a video composition.
- **Clip 01 purpose:** make the viewer curious about the dancer's ability to keep two plates balanced.
- **Storyboard (10 s):** 0-2 extreme close-up of two plates on the palms, plates moving subtly with the wrists. 2-4 camera slowly pulls back, revealing the focused face. 4-7 she raises both plates gradually while opening her hands. 7-10 she starts the arm-extension movement with a stable body.
- **Voice-over:** "Dua piring. Satu keseimbangan. Bisakah tetap bertahan saat tubuh mulai menari?"
- **Audio decision for the clip:** no generated speech or music; voice-over is added in editing.

## 3. Findings

**ChatGPT-written prompt.** The prompt reversed the storyboard's camera logic (full-body opening and push-in instead of close-up and pull-back), so the hook was a wide stage with a small figure and the voice-over "Dua piring" had no visible plates. Plates were cropped at the end, and a 17-line negative list accompanied almost no visible skill demonstration. The start image actually used was not stated.

**Claude v1.** The storyboard sequence was followed in one take with a strong close-up opening. Failures: plates cropped by the frame edge for about 3 s at the end, the camera kept pulling back past the requested stop, about 2 s of near-static footage at the end, and weak visible balance demonstration.

**Claude v2.** Improvements: a valid full-body end frame (about 68% of frame height, feet and floor visible, both plates inside the frame with roughly 8 to 10% margin), visible plate tilt and recovery, a face fully visible by about 4 s, no static hold. Failures: plates cropped or out of frame from about 4.5 s to 8 s (both essentially out of frame near 6 s) because the arms opened while the camera was still tight; camera stop about 1 to 1.5 s later than requested; the left plate tilted close to vertical around 8.5 s (physical plausibility unverified from stills); opening background differed from the written spec.
