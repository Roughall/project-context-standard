# Channel Pivot and Niche Separation — Decision Checkpoint v1.0

- Date: 2026-11-06
- Status: IN_PROGRESS
- Scope: Existing Video_AI YouTube channel and proposed Bandaro Betta channel.
- Type: Strategy checkpoint; recommendation based on audience/brand fit, not a guaranteed prediction of platform distribution.

## 1. Concern

The existing Video_AI channel has a history of AI animal stories, characters, animation, and entertainment. Publishing a materially different niche—betta fish education, hobby content, community, and commerce—may create an audience mismatch. The degree of mismatch has not yet been measured from current channel analytics.

## 2. Working principles

- Existing audience and viewing history can influence which viewers initially respond to new uploads.
- YouTube does not permanently lock a channel to one niche; a prior content history does not prove that a pivot must fail.
- A drop in views alone does not establish a shadowban or prove that a channel is irrecoverable.
- These are strategic working principles, not guarantees about YouTube recommendations.

## 3. Recommended direction

**Do not delete the existing Video_AI channel.** Preserve its videos, subscribers, historical performance data, and remaining audience value.

If the audience promise remains materially different, establish Bandaro Betta as a separate channel:
- Video_AI: AI storytelling, animal characters, animation, and entertainment.
- Bandaro Betta: betta fish discovery, reliable education, individual fish documentation, hobby community, and transparent commerce.

The rationale is audience and brand separation—not a belief that YouTube permanently locks channels into one niche.

## 4. Validation plan

For Bandaro Betta:
1. Establish a clear channel identity and consistent audience promise.
2. Publish an initial batch of approximately 10–15 videos across a small number of consistent pilot formats: Betta Detective, One Fish, One Story, and What Would You Choose?
3. Evaluate the batch as a whole, not from one or two uploads.
4. Track Shorts Feed distribution, viewed versus swiped away, average view duration / percentage viewed, new and returning viewers where available, subscriber conversion, meaningful engagement, channel visits, shop clicks, inquiries, orders, and production effort.
5. Compare formats and look for repeatable patterns. The batch is an initial learning experiment, not a guarantee of success.

For Video_AI:
- Preserve the channel and its historical analytics.
- Review recent upload performance, traffic sources, returning viewers, audience overlap, and whether older videos still receive meaningful distribution before deciding whether to continue, pause, or gradually pivot.
- Do not label it shadowbanned without stronger evidence.

## 5. Decision boundaries

- Deleting the existing channel is not approved by this checkpoint.
- A separate Bandaro Betta channel is recommended if the audience and content promise remain materially different.
- A gradual pivot of the existing channel remains an option if analytics show meaningful audience overlap or the team later chooses to redefine Video_AI's brand.
- No claim is made that 10–15 uploads guarantee learning or success.

## 6. Next actions

1. Confirm that the channel under discussion is the Video_AI channel publishing animal stories and AI animation, including Bobo and The Pizza Thief.
2. Review its latest YouTube Studio analytics before any pivot or account-level decision.
3. Define Bandaro Betta's channel identity and prepare its first pilot batch.
4. Reassess after the pilot using observed audience and business outcomes.
