## 4. Shared patterns (hypotheses)

1. **Cut cadence about every 2 to 3 seconds,** the first cut before 6 s, no shot longer than about 6 s.
2. **Shot-size mix:** extreme close-up or macro, close-up, medium, wide two-shot, reaction cutaway, prop or inset.
3. **Variety is not only location.** R-A used five settings; R-B used one set with many angles and insets.
4. **Two characters in dialogue.** The second host asks the viewer's question, which keeps a question open and leads into the CTA.
5. **Visual proof for each claim** (cutaway scenes that demonstrate the idea; inset images that show results).
6. **Text system:** captions of one to three words, key terms enlarged, numbered chapter labels, a keyword CTA card for 3 to 4 s at the end, a small account watermark.
7. **Audio:** dialogue (about 84 to 88% of the time active) over a continuous low-frequency bed (the quietest 10% of the audio stays near -26 to -28 dB, and 57 to 62% of the energy is below 250 Hz), integrated loudness about -15 LUFS.
8. **Chapters and a mid-roll cue** (numbered points; a save icon about 9 s in) in the longer reference.

## 6. Application options (budget-aware)

DOLA allows about one 10-second generation per clip per account, so variety is cheapest in this order:

1. **Re-edit the existing 30.2 s with no new credits:** punch-ins of 130 to 150% for close-ups of plates, face, feet, and hands (the 1080x1920 export is upscaled from 720x1280, so more than about 150% will look soft); still-image inserts from the character sheet (brooch, embroidery, suntiang, pose sequence) with slow zoom; word-by-word captions; numbered chapter labels; a keyword CTA; a licensed low bed; loudness toward about -14 LUFS.
2. **Several angles per generation (Mode M, planned cuts).** Misi Mustahil Klip B produced about four cuts in one generation. Mode M is **not approved**; this analysis is a reason to consider it, and the decision is pending. A different start image per generation would also provide different backgrounds.
3. **Two-host explainer format** reusing the existing dance clips as cutaways, with the second host asking the viewer's question and leading into the CTA. Cultural facts must be verified from sources before publication.

## 7. Proposed standard (PROPOSED, not adopted)

S1 average shot length of about 3.5 s or less for short 9:16 videos, the first cut before 6 s; S2 a mix of at least four shot sizes; S3 either several settings or one set with at least four angles plus inserts; S4 visual proof for each claim; S5 captions of one to three words with a consistent safe zone; S6 a keyword CTA for 3 to 4 s at the end and an optional mid-roll cue; S7 a continuous bed under dialogue or voice-over with loudness about -15 to -14 LUFS; S8 delivery at 1080x1920 (the references are 480x360 and are not format templates).

These are thresholds read from two videos of one niche. They should be tested by A/B on the Tari Piring re-edit against the current version, comparing retention at 3 s and at the end, average view duration, and completion, using platform analytics.

## 8. Limitations and open items

- Two videos, one creator, one niche; no performance data for either; the reasons for their success are not known.
- Shot detection is approximate; text and speech were read from low-resolution frames or inferred from timing.
- Open: owner decision on Mode M; choice of re-edit versus new production; verification of cultural facts for any voice-over or captions.
