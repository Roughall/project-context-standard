# Bandaro Betta.ID — Indonesian 15-Second Content Niche Checkpoint v1.0

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Status:** IN_PROGRESS
- **Scope:** Temporary/working niche decision for short-form content; hypotheses pending published pilot evidence.
- **Brand:** Bandaro Betta.ID (`@bandaro_betta.id`)
- **Primary format:** Vertical short video, target duration 15 seconds.
- **Audience:** General Indonesian audience, including people who are not currently betta hobbyists.
- **Tone:** Mixed humor — receh, relatable, light satire, and absurd — using casual everyday Indonesian.

## 1. Approved working niche

**CUPANG, TINGKAH, & TINGKAT KEHALUAN**

Audience promise: **“Ikannya cupang. Kelakuannya orang kita.”**

Bandaro Betta.ID is positioned as an entertainment-led betta fish account. Betta fish are the visual stars and recurring product; the comedic context comes from familiar Indonesian everyday habits, spending dilemmas, social situations, preferences, and playful visual surprises.

This is a working niche decision, not a claim that the format is unique or proven to perform. Reassess after a small published pilot batch.

## 2. Creative principles

- Understandable immediately without background knowledge of betta fish.
- One joke, question, or visual surprise per 15-second video.
- Casual Indonesian phrasing; avoid formal narration, heavy themes, niche jargon, and complicated fictional worlds.
- Real footage of actual fish is the source of truth for appearance, condition, behavior, and availability.
- Humor should feel familiar and inclusive; do not stereotype a region, ethnicity, religion, class, or generation.
- The fish remains central; human situations are a relatable framing device.
- Sales information is clear and honest, never dependent on fake scarcity or misleading claims.

## 3. Five recurring series

### A. DOMPET VS CUPANG
Everyday Indonesian spending humor: tanggal tua, gajian, checkout, saving promises, and temptation.
- Examples: “Tanggal tua, jangan buka marketplace”; “Niatnya nabung, malah nambah cupang.”
- Commerce role: introduce real available fish with stock ID and price/DM route.

### B. TINGKAH ORANG + TINGKAH CUPANG
Lightly map familiar human habits onto expressive fish footage, clearly as comedy rather than biological claims.
- Examples: “Ketemu saingan baru”; “Merasa paling cakep satu tongkrongan.”
- Commerce role: build recall for a featured individual fish and its stock ID.

### C. PILIHAN WARGA
Simple preference choices between real fish, colors, or fin styles. No specialist knowledge required.
- Examples: “Kalau boleh bawa pulang satu, pilih mana?”; “Tim warna kalem atau mencolok?”
- Commerce role: learn preferences; include IDs and allow direct purchase inquiries without forcing a comment first.

### D. CUPANG SOK SULTAN
Playful parody of confidence, prestige, and dramatic presence.
- Examples: “Jalan pelan, biar semua orang lihat”; “Pembawaan sudah CEO.”
- Commerce role: feature visually distinctive/premium-positioned fish, including Crowntail Black Orchid, without unsupported quality or price claims.

### E. LAH, KOK BAGUS BANGET?
Visual-first short clips that reveal real fish colors, fins, or movement.
- Examples: “Awalnya cuma lewat”; “Tunggu sampai dia buka ekor.”
- Commerce role: showcase accurate footage of fish currently available.

## 4. 15-second episode structure

A flexible template, not a rigid rule:
- **0–2s — Hook:** immediately recognizable Indonesian line or visual.
- **2–6s — Setup:** show the fish and establish the situation.
- **6–11s — Payoff:** punchline, visual reveal, or decisive choice.
- **11–15s — Conversion:** show actual fish ID and one clear action (e.g. DM for price/availability).

For interaction-led episodes, the primary CTA may be a simple A/B/C choice. Do not force a sales CTA into every video if it damages the premise; ensure interested viewers can still find the actual stock.

## 5. First pilot batch

Produce three pilots first, preferably in one recording session:
1. **“Tanggal tua, jangan buka marketplace”** — DOMPET VS CUPANG; feature an available Halfmoon.
2. **“Anak sultan masuk tongkrongan”** — CUPANG SOK SULTAN; feature actual Crowntail Black Orchid stock.
3. **“Dikasih gratis, pilih satu!”** — PILIHAN WARGA; compare three actual available fish with clear labels.

Use actual footage and real stock IDs. The IDs in brainstorming drafts (HM01, BO01, etc.) are placeholders and must be replaced with the live inventory IDs before publishing.

## 6. Product and inventory alignment

Current inventory mix as reported by the owner:
- Halfmoon: approximately 70%.
- Plakat: approximately 25%.
- Crowntail: approximately 5%.
- Crowntail Black Orchid has historically been the best seller, according to the owner.

Implication: use Black Orchid as a distinctive attention/brand feature, but do not let the content plan overrepresent it. Halfmoon should carry most product exposure because it represents most available stock; Plakat should have recurring representation too. Match every product CTA to current availability.

## 7. Sales path and measurement

**Path:** short video → fish ID / profile visit → direct DM inquiry → send current fish video, price, condition, and shipping details → record order and update stock.

Measure by series, not by isolated viral views:
- viewed vs. swiped away and retention/average percentage viewed where available;
- completion/replay where available;
- profile visits;
- DMs asking for price or availability;
- qualified inquiries and completed orders attributed to each video;
- production time per video.

A format is not validated by views or comments alone. Compare multiple pilots and track actual purchase intent and sales.

## 8. Production constraints and editorial guardrails

- Target one 15-second video per day at the start; batch-record footage to reduce effort.
- No expensive sets, large cast, or complicated story worlds required.
- Keep text short, legible, and conversational.
- Do not materially alter fish color, fin shape, size, condition, or behavior with AI/editing.
- AI may support captions, ideation, or clearly stylized backgrounds, but must not misrepresent the real sale fish.
- Do not provoke or stress fish for engagement.
- Avoid unsupported care/health claims and fake scarcity.
- Display current stock ID and availability accurately.
- No guaranteed virality or conversion claims.

## 9. Decision and next action

**Working decision approved:** general-audience Indonesian betta entertainment, mixed humor, casual language, 15-second short videos, sales-oriented but entertainment-led.

**Next action:** produce and publish the three pilot videos above, then compare retention, profile visits, qualified DMs, and sales. Keep this niche provisional until pilot evidence supports retaining or revising it.
